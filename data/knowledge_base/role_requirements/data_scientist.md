# Data Scientist — Typical Role Expectations

GENERAL REFERENCE CONTEXT about the role archetype - not a substitute for
any specific job description's actual stated requirements.

## Typical core skills

- Statistics fundamentals (hypothesis testing, distributions, confidence
  intervals) applied to real business questions, not just theory.
- Data manipulation (Pandas, SQL) and the ability to clean genuinely
  messy real-world data.
- Classical ML (scikit-learn, XGBoost) and knowing when a simpler model
  is the right choice over a more complex one.
- Communicating findings to non-technical stakeholders clearly, often via
  visualization.

## Commonly expected, but varies by specific job

- Experiment design (A/B testing) and understanding common pitfalls
  (peeking, multiple comparisons, selection bias).
- Deep learning fundamentals, if the role involves unstructured data
  (images, text, audio).
- Production ML experience - getting a model from a notebook into an
  actual running system, which is a very different skill from building
  the model itself.

## What typically distinguishes strong candidates in interviews

Being able to walk through a project end-to-end, including what didn't
work and what they changed, rather than just presenting a polished final
result. Strong candidates can defend their modeling choices and explain
what they'd do differently with more time or data, not just describe the
final pipeline.
