# Machine Learning Interview Questions (Reference Bank)

## Fundamentals

- Explain the bias-variance trade-off in your own words, with an example
  from a project you've worked on.
- How do you decide whether a model is overfitting, and what would you do
  about it?
- What's the difference between precision and recall, and when would you
  optimize for one over the other?

## Applied / project-specific

- Walk me through your feature engineering choices for [a specific
  project] - why did you pick those features?
- How did you validate that your model's performance would hold up on
  data it hadn't seen during training?
- If your model's predictions started degrading in production, how would
  you investigate why?

## Classical ML vs. deep learning judgment

- For [a specific project], why did you choose a classical ML approach
  (e.g. XGBoost) over a deep learning approach, or vice versa?
- What are the practical trade-offs (data requirements, interpretability,
  training cost) between the two approaches?

## Evaluation and metrics

- How do you evaluate a classification model when the classes are
  imbalanced (e.g. fraud detection, where fraudulent transactions are
  rare)?
- What's a confusion matrix, and what does each quadrant tell you about
  your model's failure modes?

## Explainability

- How would you explain a specific prediction your model made to a
  non-technical stakeholder?
- What tools or techniques have you used (or would you use) for model
  explainability (e.g. SHAP, feature importance)?
