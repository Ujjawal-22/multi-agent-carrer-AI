# Behavioral Interview Questions (Reference Bank)

These should always be grounded in the candidate's actual stated
experience (an internship, a specific project, a team setting) rather
than asked as disconnected generic prompts.

## Grounded in internship/work experience

- Tell me about a time during your internship when something didn't work
  as expected - what did you do?
- Describe a piece of feedback you received during your internship that
  changed how you approached your work.
- What was the most technically challenging part of your internship, and
  how did you work through it?

## Grounded in project work

- Tell me about a decision you made on [a specific project] that you'd
  make differently if you started over today.
- Describe a time you had to learn a new technology quickly to finish a
  project - how did you approach it?
- What's something about [a specific project] you're genuinely proud of,
  beyond just "it worked"?

## Collaboration and communication

- Describe a time you had to explain a technical concept to someone
  without a technical background.
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with an approach a teammate or
  mentor suggested - how did you handle it?

## Growth and self-awareness

- What's a skill you're actively working on improving right now, and
  what are you doing about it?
- Looking at the gap between your current skills and what this role
  requires, what's your plan for closing it?
