Compare the candidate's resume data against the job's requirements below.
You must NOT invent, assume, or credit the candidate with any skill that
does not actually appear in the resume data provided — if it's not there,
it's missing, even if it seems like a "reasonable assumption" for someone
with their background.

Classify every skill/technology mentioned in the job requirements into
exactly one category:
- matching_skills: the skill (or a clear equivalent) appears directly in

- missing_skills: no matching, partial, or transferable evidence exists

- reason: a brief, specific explanation of why this skill matters for
  this particular role.
- suggested_learning_priority: "high", "medium", or "low", based on how
  central the skill is to the role and how foundational it likely is.
